🧩 Riddle of the Day (Cyber Edition)
A daily brain exercise focused on digital forensics and cybersecurity concepts — designed to sharpen analytical thinking and keep investigative skills fresh.

💬 Quote of the Day
Automatically updated and centered around growth, discipline, and early-career resilience in DFIR. Because mindset matters just as much as methodology.

📈 Opportunities Page
A space dedicated to training events, internships, certifications, and growth opportunities to help investigators move forward intentionally.

📰 Newsfeed Page
A central spot to keep up with what’s happening in the DFIR world, pulling in updates from blogs and industry news sources.

🤝 Community Board
A collaborative area where analysts can ask questions, share insights, and contribute knowledge. The goal is simple: build together.

🛤️ The Investigator’s Path
A space where individuals — regardless of rank or years of experience — can submit articles, insights, or career reflections as another way to connect and support others in the field.

This project has been developed in partnership with ChatGPT, which assisted with concept development and technical implementation throughout the build.

If you see anything that could be improved or have ideas for features you’d like to see added, I would truly appreciate your feedback.
